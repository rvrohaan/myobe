Refund & Cancellation Policy

Effective date: 15 June 2026

This policy explains how token purchases on myobe.in (operated by My OBE) are handled.

Refunds

You may request a refund for unused token credits within 7 days of purchase. Tokens that have been used (wholly or partially) are non-refundable. Refunds, where approved, are processed to the original payment method within 5-7 business days.

Cancellation

You may stop using the Service at any time. There is no recurring subscription to cancel; purchases are one-time token top-ups.

How to request

Email help@myobe.in with your account email and payment reference.

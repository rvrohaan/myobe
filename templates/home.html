Who we serve

Built for every professional college

Accreditation and documentation requirements differ across disciplines — MyOBE adapts to your field, delivering integrated OBE-SDG reports for each.

Engineering
Built around the NBA & AICTE outcome-based framework — COs, POs/PSOs, CO–PO mapping, attainment, and Bloom’s taxonomy alignment for every course.
Management
Generates NBA/NAAC-ready course documentation for MBA & BBA programs — program outcomes, CO mapping, and assessment rubrics tuned to management curricula.
Pharmacy
Aligned to PCI norms and NBA accreditation — D.Pharm, B.Pharm, and M.Pharm course outcomes, mapping, and lesson plans in the format inspectors expect.
Medical
Supports NMC competency-based medical education (CBME) — structured learning objectives, competencies, and assessment blueprints for MBBS and allied health courses.
Dental
Tailored to DCI requirements — competency-based outcomes, lesson plans, and question banks for BDS and MDS programs.
Law
Maps to BCI and NAAC expectations — course outcomes, program outcomes, and assessment documentation for LLB, BA-LLB, and LLM courses.
Architecture & Planning
Aligned to COA and NBA frameworks — studio and theory course outcomes, CO–PO mapping, and attainment tracking for B.Arch and planning programs.
Agriculture & Allied Sciences
Built for ICAR and NAAC norms — outcome-based documentation, lesson plans, and assessment design for agriculture, horticulture, and allied science courses.
Science (Degree Colleges)
Aligned to the UGC LOCF framework and NAAC accreditation — programme outcomes, CO–PO mapping, and attainment tracking for B.Sc and M.Sc degree programs.
Arts (Degree Colleges)
Built for UGC LOCF graduate attributes and NAAC norms — course outcomes, programme-outcome mapping, and assessment documentation for B.A and M.A humanities and social science programs.

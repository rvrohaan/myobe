What we offer

Every document your faculty needs

Each tool produces submission-ready output aligned with NBA, NAAC, and OBE frameworks.

📘

CO Generator

Generate Bloom's taxonomy-aligned Course Outcome statements from any syllabus — formatted and export-ready.

📚

Question Bank Generator

Auto-generate 2-mark, 5-mark, and 10-mark questions, assignments, and quizzes — CO-tagged, export-ready.

📋

Question Paper Generator

Compose Assignment, Quiz, Internal Assessment, or Final Exam papers — with custom marks and CO coverage.

CO–PO Mapping

Generate an NBA-compliant CO–PO / CO–PSO mapping table with 3/2/1/0 attainment scores.

Course Lesson Plan
